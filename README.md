<p>
🚀 My journey started at university with a love for building user interfaces. Since then it has grown into building whole products: from the first question ("what is the real problem here?") all the way to production. 〜(꒪꒳꒪)〜

🧡 These days I'm a <b>Frontend Developer / AI Product Engineer</b> at <b>Classeh</b>. For 3+ years I've been taking features end to end: problem framing, UX, frontend architecture, backend APIs, release and follow-up.

🎡 Still inquisitive by nature: I love turning messy feedback into clear specs, and then into things people actually enjoy using.

🤖 I work in an AI-native way (Claude Code with custom skills, subagents and MCP), but I review and test everything I ship. :D
</p>

<h3 align="left">What I'm building:</h3>

- 🌌 **Uryva**: an international, AI-powered knowledge platform. I'm its product engineer in practice: about 93% of the frontend, 540+ releases, 12 languages (RTL included), plus a good chunk of the NestJS backend.
- 🏙️ **Fahmyar**: a gamified K-12 learning app with a 3D open-world city and 11 Three.js learning games, tuned to run smoothly on low-end phones.
- 📞 **Classeh Target**: a sales CRM PWA with AI call summaries, next-best-action hints and one-tap messaging.
- 🏫 **Classeh LMS**: a micro-frontend school platform (finance, report cards, a real-time messenger).

<h3 align="left">Latest side project:</h3>

🏠 **[Torob Khaneh](https://github.com/kiana-nb/torob-khaneh)**: a "Torob for homes" demo I built for Torob's AI Product Engineer challenge. You search for an apartment in plain Persian, and it finds real Tehran listings, merges duplicates and explains its ranking.
👉 **[Live demo](https://torob-khaneh.vercel.app)** · **[Demo video](https://torob-khaneh.vercel.app/demo/)** · **[How it was built](https://torob-khaneh.vercel.app/about/)**

 🌱 I'm currently leveling up my **product practice** (discovery, design thinking, decision-making) and playing with **3D on the web**.
